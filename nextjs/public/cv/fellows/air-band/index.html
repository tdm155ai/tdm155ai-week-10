Settings

How to play — filter

  1. Your right hand is a low-pass: pinch thumb and index together to muffle the band, spread them to open it up.
  2. Your left hand is a high-pass: pinch to thin out the bass, spread to bring it back.
  3. Raise your hands for more resonance (a sharper "wah"). Drop your hands out of view and the filter opens again.

How to play — drums

  1. Point a finger into a pad at the top of your column to hit it; leave and come back to hit again.
  2. Tap down with an open palm for a deeper hit. Harder and faster is louder.
  3. Swipe an open palm sideways to change drums: kit, bongos, congas, electro.
  4. Pinch thumb to index to start a loop; pinch again to close it and it keeps playing.

How to play — guitar

  1. Swing your strumming hand's index fingertip all the way through the shaded strings zone. Down and up both play; faster is louder.
  2. To skip a stroke, bring your hand back around the side of the strings instead of through them.
  3. Your other hand picks the chord by how many fingers you hold up (fist, 1–4, open hand).

How to play — piano

  1. Hold both hands up like they're resting on piano keys. Each finger is its own note — middle fingers are C.
  2. Press a finger down (bend it, like pressing a key) to play its note; lift it to let go.
  3. Faster presses play louder. If notes trigger by accident, raise Press amount needed.

How to play — trumpet

  1. Bring one hand to your mouth as if holding the mouthpiece.
  2. Put the other hand in front of it, like holding the valves.
  3. It sounds while you hold the pose. Curl fingers on the far hand to change notes.
