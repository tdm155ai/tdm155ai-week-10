Hand drums

Point a finger and tap down, or tap with an open palm. Swipe an open palm sideways to change drums. Pinch to start and stop a loop. Make a fist over a loop at the bottom to grab it; open your hand outside the tray to throw it away.

Everything runs on this computer. The camera image is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Keys (no camera)

A S D F hit pads (Shift = palm) · ← → change drums · R or Space = loop · Del clears loops
