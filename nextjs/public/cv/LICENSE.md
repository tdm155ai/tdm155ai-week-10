# Sample clips for /draw-a-box

Both clips were generated for this repo on 2026-11-11 with ffmpeg and ImageMagick (no third-party footage), and are free to use (CC0):

- `bars.mp4`: ffmpeg's `smptehdbars` test pattern with a white bar sweeping across, 640×360, 6 s.
- `title.mp4`: ffmpeg's `gradients` source with a "DRAW A BOX" title card drawn in ImageMagick, 640×360, 6 s.

/draw-a-box also uses `gradient.mp4`, `fractal.mp4`, and `life.mp4` from `/samples/` (see `public/samples/LICENSE.md`).
