# Samples

Every file here was made for this repo on 2026-11-11 (ffmpeg's built-in generators and hand-written SVG), so there is nothing to license: use them however you like. Replace them with your own media in the repo-root `_media/` folder (served at `/media/...`).

- Sounds: `beep-a.mp3` (440 Hz, 2 s), `ping.mp3` (880 Hz blip), `thump.mp3` (low hit), `chord.mp3` (A major, 8 s, fades in and out), `wind.mp3` (filtered pink noise, 10 s).
- Video (8 s, 640×360, silent, loops): `fractal.mp4` (Mandelbrot zoom), `gradient.mp4` (drifting color), `life.mp4` (Game of Life).
- Images: `dawn.jpg`, `fractal.jpg`, `moon.svg`, `sun.svg`, `rose.svg`.
