So far most of what we've made has been for screens. This week is about a physical space and a real audience: launching sound, video, images, and light at the right moment, the way a theater, an opera, or a concert does. The first hour, everyone builds a Hello World version of each mechanic at their own computer. Then groups make a short live performance with the mechanics they've got.

## The mechanics

- **[Launch](/launch):** fire media with keys, on-screen buttons, and MIDI controllers (drum pads, keys, dials), from a cue list with a Go button.
- **[Voice cues](/voice):** read a text aloud (a sonnet, say) and things pop up at the cue words. Live transcription, and cues that follow the script.
- **[Track](/track):** MediaPipe in the browser: hands, faces, and poses as triggers and controllers.
- **[Draw a box](/draw-a-box):** under the overhead camera, draw a box on paper and a video plays inside it.
- **[Pro apps](/pro-apps):** QLab, TouchDesigner, Isadora, and the rest: what each one's maneuver is, and which ones you could build yourself with Claude Code.
