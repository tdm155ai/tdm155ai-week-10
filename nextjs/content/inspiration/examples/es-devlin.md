**Who:** a British artist and stage designer whose sets are sculptures that light and projection transform: rotating cubes, revolving worlds, text as architecture. Her video art appeared in U2's Sphere residency (see [U2 at Sphere](/inspiration/examples/u2-sphere)).

**Why it's here:** she thinks like a theater designer in a stadium: the object first, then what light and projection do to it. That's this week's argument for a physical space over a screen.
