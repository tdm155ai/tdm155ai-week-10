*Researched with AI (Claude), November 11, 2026.*

**What:** a nightly show projecting light, color, and pattern onto the gothic interior of Notre-Dame Basilica in Montreal, with an orchestral score: the architecture is the screen. (A version also ran at Grace Cathedral in San Francisco.)

**Why it's here:** projection mapping that serves a room rather than covering it. The building's ribs and arches decide where every image goes.
