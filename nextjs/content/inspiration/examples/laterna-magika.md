*Researched with AI (Claude), November 11, 2026.*

**What:** created by scenographer Josef Svoboda and director Alfréd Radok for Expo 58 in Brussels, Laterna Magika mixes live actors and dancers with film projected on moving screens, timed so performers seem to pass between stage and screen. It's now a company of Prague's National Theatre.

**Why it's here:** the grandparent of everything this week. Cues fired so precisely that live and projected bodies interact, decades before computers ran the show.
