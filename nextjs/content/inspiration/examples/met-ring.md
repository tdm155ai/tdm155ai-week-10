*Researched with AI (Claude), November 11, 2026.*

**What:** Robert Lepage and Ex Machina's *Ring* (2010–12): 24 planks on a central axis that turn into a river, a mountain, a staircase, a horse, with projection, some of it interactive, mapped onto them as they move.

**Why it's here:** projection on a moving set, where mapping and stage machinery have to agree, and opera as the original "multiple forms in one" that Marlon points to for the final party.
