**What:** U2:UV Achtung Baby Live, the 2023–24 residency that opened Sphere. Credits (PLSN, Wikipedia): creative director Willie Williams; content by Treatment Studio, with video art by Es Devlin, Marco Brambilla, John Gerrard, and ILM; stage by Stufish.

**Why it's here:** the screen is the room. Content had to be designed for a dome, from the audience's eye, so previsualization (see [media server](/pro-apps/mechanics/media-server)) is the whole job.
