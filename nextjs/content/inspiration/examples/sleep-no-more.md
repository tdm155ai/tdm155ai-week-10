**What:** Punchdrunk's wordless, immersive *Macbeth* (with film noir and Hitchcock), produced in New York by Emursive from 2011. The audience roams a "hotel" of about a hundred rooms; the show loops, and sound and light change in every room in sync with the performers.

**Why it's here:** a show you walk through. Cueing isn't one stage and one Go; it's many rooms running at once, which is closer to an installation, and to what a group of you could build across the studio.
