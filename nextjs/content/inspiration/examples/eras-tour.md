---
title: "The Eras Tour"
description: "Taylor Swift's 2023–24 stadium tour: a long LED wall, an LED stage floor, lifts, and 50,000 synchronized wristbands."
---

**Who designed it** (Wikipedia and the OTRA credits database): **Ethan Tobman**, creative director and production designer; **Baz Halpin** (Silent House Group), production designer and lighting designer, with Silent House also credited for content and VFX; **Eric Marchwinski** (Earlybird Visual), associate lighting designer and lighting programmer. PixMob made the LED wristbands.

**Why it's here:** three hours, 40-plus songs, the same show night after night: the opposite of Phish. Everything is cued, much of it to timecode; the lighting programmer's job is building that cue stack.
