Concerts first: they are what most of you will design for. Each entry is one line on why it's here; the **examples** pages go deeper, and the **case studies** take a show apart into its mechanics and suggest how you'd build a small version of each with Claude Code.

- **[Examples](/inspiration/examples):** one page per show, studio, or artist.
- **[Case studies](/inspiration/case-studies):** a show's tools and mechanics, and how you'd code each piece.

## Concerts and tours

- [Phish, Live at Sphere | Exclusive Behind-the-Scenes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aP-y5CDJBUs) (Moment Factory, 5:46): a different, live-driven show every night on a giant LED dome. → [example](/inspiration/examples/phish-at-sphere), [case study](/inspiration/case-studies/phish-at-sphere)

- [Shows & Concerts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WD8zBDsf1Kg) (Moment Factory, 1:14): a one-minute reel of the Montreal studio's concert work. → [Moment Factory](/inspiration/examples/moment-factory)
- [Infinite Content Tour ∞ Arcade Fire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QDoNZKVGkk) (Moment Factory, 2:04): 360° creative direction for an arena tour.
- [Nine Inch Nails, Festival Tour 2013 | Interactivity & Live Show Content](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nORTQidJ0_g) (Moment Factory, 1:04): visuals driven by tracking the performers: today's `/track`, at festival scale.
- [Behind U2's Residency at Sphere Las Vegas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=El-a0rbmAIA) (Apple Music, 31:18): watch the art-direction and visuals chapter at 8:52. → [example](/inspiration/examples/u2-sphere)
- [Taylor Swift's Eras Tour: A Deep Dive into the Tech Behind the Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qmqXbggxFY) (Live Production Mastery, third-party, 38:46): a 3D walk through the stage, LED floor, and lifts. → [example](/inspiration/examples/eras-tour), [case study](/inspiration/case-studies/eras-tour)
- [How Concert LED Wristbands Work | WSJ Tech Behind](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCsmZA08oD8) (The Wall Street Journal, 5:29): the audience as the display; one cue fired at 50,000 wrists. → [case study](/inspiration/case-studies/crowd-wristbands)
- [Behind the scenes of Coldplay's Music of the Spheres World Tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7OHTA-N0yM) (Disguise, 4:23): the screens director at front of house running disguise and Notch. → [example](/inspiration/examples/coldplay)
- [Abstract: The Art of Design | Es Devlin: Stage Design | FULL EPISODE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jo4aAVjuh2o) (Netflix, 42:20, free): the stage designer behind U2, Beyoncé, and the opera house, on her process. → [example](/inspiration/examples/es-devlin)
