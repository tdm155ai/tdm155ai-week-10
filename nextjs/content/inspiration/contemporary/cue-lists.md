## WICKED (Broadway): the production stage manager calling the show

- **Video:** [WICKED In Action: The Production Stage Manager | WICKED the Musical](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FEVb6tgzJ40) (WICKED The Musical, 1:19, 2026-10-07; official)

- **Mechanic:** cue list
- **Why it's here:** Wicked is huge right now, and this shows a human pressing "go" on every moment.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/launch/cue-list](/launch/cue-list) already does this: Space = Go, and the next cue stands by.
- **Credits and sources:** PSM named in the video description (link above).
