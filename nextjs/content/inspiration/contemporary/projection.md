## Back to the Future (Broadway): effects revealed

- **Video:** [See How BACK TO THE FUTURE On Broadway Creates Thrilling Effects](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hrPmF-0Sxkw) (Theatrely, 4:39, 2024-04-19; theater press)
- **Watch at:** 1:30. Rehearsal footage of a neon light-line staircase around a performer (watched)
- **Mechanic:** projection, theater mechanics
- **Why it's here:** The DeLorean, done live.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/draw-a-box](/draw-a-box). Draw a staircase on paper, and a cue lights its outline on [/stage](/stage).
- **Credits and sources:** Video designer Finn Ross (unverified; not confirmed in a source that was opened). Also [Back To The Future Musical | Lighting Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8dzdwOc-J3Q) (Harvey Lighting, 4:55, 2024-02-26; third-party previz in Capture, ETC Eos; metadata only).

## Stranger Things: The First Shadow: illusions and video

- **Video:** [Illusions & Technical Effects of Stranger Things: The First Shadow | 2025 Special Tony Award](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8navrC74bQA) (The Tony Awards, 2:08, 2025-06-03; official)
- **Watch at:** metadata only
- **Mechanic:** projection, theater mechanics
- **Why it's here:** Netflix's biggest show, now with stage magic.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/stage](/stage). Pair a red neon "swipe" cue with a sound cue (like the show's "szchuum").
- **Credits and sources:** Illusions by Jamie Harrison and Chris Fisher; technical design by Gary Beestone and Edward Pierce (Tony video description). Set/video by Miriam Buether and Benjamin Pearcy of 59 Productions; effects include the red neon swipes with a sound cue and smoke that's part real, part video ([New York Theatre Guide](https://www.newyorktheatreguide.com/theatre-news/news/designing-the-upside-down-in-stranger-things-the-first-shadow-on-broadway)). Backstage tour: [Go Behind-the-Scenes at STRANGER THINGS: THE FIRST SHADOW with Burke Swanson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJUqUboxuuc) (Broadwaycom, 9:06, 2025-10-10; metadata only).
