*Researched with AI for this class and checked on the morning of November 11, 2026 (title, channel, length, date, and embed status read from each YouTube page). **watched** means the moment was seen on screen at that timestamp; **metadata only** means the timestamp is a description chapter or a rough pointer. Credits come from the linked press or studio pages; anything not confirmed is marked **unverified**.*

## Couldn't verify

- No official production or behind-the-scenes video found for the Brat tour, the GUTS tour, the Short n' Sweet tour, Chromakopia, the Cowboy Carter tour, Mayhem Ball, Doja Cat's Scarlet tour, SZA's SOS tour or BLACKPINK. Fan full-show uploads exist for all of them, but none show the rig.
- Credits found in text only: Chromakopia's production and lighting design are credited to Alex Reardon ([PLSN](https://plsn.com/archives/march-2025/tyler-the-creators-chromakopia-tour)), and the "Brutalist sea-container set" to Silent House ([Silent House](https://silent-house.com/project/tyler-the-creator-chromakopia-tour)). No video studio was named.
- The lighting designer for Hit Me Hard and Soft is not named on the Moment Factory or TPi pages.
- Sunset Blvd.: camera count and operator details (Broadway News is paywalled). Dorian Gray: the video designer's name, and whether any footage is pre-recorded.
- Rosalía's MOTOMAMI tour with on-stage camera operators: the official [SAKURA (MOTOMAMI Tour Live)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yIuBWL3071E) exists (ROSALÍA, 4:41, 2022-12-31), but it wasn't watched to confirm camera operators.
- aespa AR: we found only performance videos and fan uploads, with no official explainer of the AR.
- Kendrick's halftime: on-field text and the "GAME OVER" moment. Neither appears in the sources that were opened.
- YouTube transcripts were blocked, so timestamps marked "metadata only" are description chapters or rough pointers.

## Leads that turned out wrong or partly wrong

- **Charli xcx "text on screen":** Wallpaper says "BRAT"/"PARTY" appeared on physical drapes, and the screens showed live camera feeds. The Sweat tour shot its live feed at 24 fps with a Steadicam operator ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91230115/charli-xcx-troye-sivan-sweat-tour-design)).
- **Fred again.. "phone videos":** This is true of his 2020–21 live show (2MS Studio page). USB002 (2025–26) was the opposite: phone-free, with a kinetic fabric installation.
- **MiMU gloves:** These are real, but both the Imogen Heap and Ariana Grande examples are from 2015.
- **Fortnite/Roblox concerts:** All are real and on official channels, but 2020–21. Sabrina Carpenter's 2025 Fortnite Festival appearance is the newer one.
- **Hadestown, Six:** Only fan-made lighting edits or 2019 Tony clips turned up.
- **Katie Mitchell's "live cinema":** Only a 2016 Barbican clip and fan uploads turned up. Use Sunset Blvd. and Dorian Gray instead.
- **Billie Eilish's tour:** It's by Moment Factory, already covered on the [Moment Factory](/inspiration/examples/moment-factory) page.
- **Anyma:** Most of his visible material is from Sphere, so we used his Coachella 2026 making-of instead. Note that it's sponsored.

## Gaps

- **Lyric- or voice-triggered visuals in young-audience shows:** we found no verified example of words appearing as they're sung, driven by speech recognition. That makes [/voice](/voice) and [/voice/script](/voice/script) ahead of the field, which is worth saying in class.
- **MediaPipe or webcam tracking in a major concert:** we found only creator tutorials and art or dance pieces.
- **Recent headphone or silent-disco theater:** The best example is still The Encounter (2016).
- **Sound that follows a performer:** L-ISA covers spatial sound on tour, but we didn't find a clear "follows the performer" demo.
