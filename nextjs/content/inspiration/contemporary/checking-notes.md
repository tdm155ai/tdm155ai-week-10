## Gaps

- **Lyric- or voice-triggered visuals in young-audience shows:** we found no verified example of words appearing as they're sung, driven by speech recognition. That makes [/voice](/voice) and [/voice/script](/voice/script) ahead of the field, which is worth saying in class.
- **MediaPipe or webcam tracking in a major concert:** we found only creator tutorials and art or dance pieces.
- **Recent headphone or silent-disco theater:** The best example is still The Encounter (2016).
- **Sound that follows a performer:** L-ISA covers spatial sound on tour, but we didn't find a clear "follows the performer" demo.
