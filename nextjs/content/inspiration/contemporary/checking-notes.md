## Gaps

- **Lyric- or voice-triggered visuals in young-audience shows:** words appearing on screen as they're sung, driven by speech recognition, are rare in major shows. That makes [/voice](/voice) and [/voice/script](/voice/script) ahead of the field, which is worth saying in class.
- **MediaPipe or webcam tracking in a major concert:** it shows up in creator tutorials and art or dance pieces, not yet on big tours.
- **Recent headphone or silent-disco theater:** The best example is still The Encounter (2016).
- **Sound that follows a performer:** L-ISA covers spatial sound on tour.
