## Holly Herndon: Holly+, singing in someone else's voice

- **Video:** [What if You Could Sing in Your Favorite Musician's Voice? | Holly Herndon | TED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5cbCYwgQkTE) (TED, 9:24, 2022-09-08; official)
- **Watch at:** the demo with the musician Pher.
- **Mechanic:** voice triggers, sound in space
- **Why it's here:** An AI voice instrument, performed live.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/voice](/voice). Spoken words change the stage, and the voice itself becomes the cue.
- **Credits and sources:** TED description.

## Charli xcx: Brat tour, words as set

- **Video:** [Charli XCX BRAT Tour Act 1 - 365, 360, Von Dutch, Rewind - London O2 Arena 28 Nov 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0S0MeCaJokw) (Saint Daniel, 11:18, 2024-11-28; third-party fan footage)
- **Watch at:** 1:26 "365" (fan's chapter)
- **Mechanic:** text as set, live camera
- **Why it's here:** Brat green and one word in Arial. The defining visual of the moment.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/voice](/voice). Each recognized lyric word fills the [/stage](/stage) in lowercase Arial on green.
- **Credits and sources:** Creative director Imogene Strauss; stage and lighting by Jonny Kingsbury / Cour Design ([Wallpaper*](https://wallpaper.com/art/exhibitions-shows/bringing-brat-to-life-the-designers-behind-charli-xcxs-victory-lap-tour)). Wallpaper says the words "BRAT"/"PARTY" were on **physical drapes**, and the screens carried real-time camera footage. For Coachella 2025, Cour Design used **Notch** for the live show ([Notch case study](https://www.notch.one/madewithnotch/charli-xcx-coachella-2025)). No official Brat tour video found. Unverified: whether any lyrics appeared on screen.
