## Sunset Blvd. (Jamie Lloyd): live-camera musical theater

- **Video:** ["SUNSET BOULEVARD" FEAT. TOM FRANCIS | Sunset Blvd.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgTbfYF5cHk) (Sunset Blvd., 3:18, 2024-09-20; official)
- **Watch at:** 1:40. Black-and-white close-up of two dancers, filmed live
- **Mechanic:** live camera
- **Why it's here:** Tony-winning, and it looks like a music video made live.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/stage](/stage) with a webcam layer in grayscale, switched by cue.
- **Credits and sources:** Co-video designer/cinematographer Joe Ransom; second camera operator Drew Redington on stage ([Broadway News](https://www.broadwaynews.com/how-the-live-camera-work-in-broadways-sunset-boulevard-elevates-its-theatricality/); paywalled beyond the standfirst). Also [Magic in the Making | SUNSET BLVD.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zgbQk1x4IVA) (Sunset Blvd., 3:45, 2025-05-09; official; watched at 1:00, choreographer Fabian Aloise interviewed). Unverified: the number of cameras.

## The Picture of Dorian Gray: one actor, many cameras

- **Video:** [Sarah Snook on "The Picture of Dorian Gray"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ybwHDRjnY4) (CBS Sunday Morning, 7:50, 2025-03-16; established press)
- **Mechanic:** live camera
- **Why it's here:** Shiv from Succession plays every role, edited live.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/stage](/stage). Two webcams with a cut button, plus prerecorded clips of the same performer.
- **Credits and sources:** Playbill says it's "filmed by numerous cameras," with "over 400 camera edits" and camera operators on stage. Set by Marg Horwell and David Bergman ([Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/kip-williams-says-the-picture-of-dorian-gray-is-a-love-letter-to-theatre-making-and-to-filmmaking)). Also [The Picture of Dorian Gray - Behind the Scenes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRe6ktqYd5U) (Theatre Matters, 3:48, 2022-07-06; third-party; Kip Williams and Marg Horwell). Unverified: the video designer's name and whether any footage is pre-recorded.

## Bad Bunny Super Bowl LX: where every camera was

- **Video:** [Bad Bunny's Halftime Show - Video Production Breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZcFvB6MRDM) (Electric, 13:34, 2026-03-19; third-party fan analysis)
- **Watch at:** 2:00. A top-down field map with camera positions and the live cut
- **Mechanic:** live camera
- **Why it's here:** It shows the biggest broadcast of the year as a camera plot.
- **Build a tiny version:** [/draw-a-box](/draw-a-box). Draw camera positions on paper, and each box becomes a "cut to" cue.
- **Credits and sources:** The description lists cameras: "F: Robotically controlled ground camera… S1, S2: Steadicam… X1, X2: Stadium skycams." Official show: [Bad Bunny's Apple Music Super Bowl Halftime Show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G6FuWd4wNd8) (NFL, 13:41, 2026-02-08). Making-of: [Bad Bunny Behind the Scenes Super Bowl Halftime Show! | NFL Stage Pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDjATf-Ws5k) (NFL, 23:53, 2026-06-11; official).
